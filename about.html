About me

Data, art, and practical problem-solving.

I'm Ben Raymond, a GIS Analyst based in New York City. I work with spatial data, quality assurance, database maintenance, and workflow improvement, with a focus on making complex systems more accurate and easier to use.

Outside of my professional GIS work, I enjoy experimenting with coding projects, visual art, and other creative endeavors that combine technical structure with thoughtful design. I grew up in Colorado and went to college in Montana, where my interest in the outdoors and the natural environment shaped my curiosity about spatial patterns and geographic systems. After college, I moved to New York City with a budding career in GIS, where I continue to explore the intersection of data, human geography, and the environment in my professional work.

In my personal time, I enjoy exploring the city through food, creating art, and going on outdoor adventures whenever possible. Some of my hobbies include playing kendama, nerding out about Magic: the Gathering, rock climbing, and cooking. These experiences continue to inform my perspective on the relationship between people, place, and data.
